## 2. What Forkast is

Forkast is a food diary. You tell it what you ate; it estimates the calories
from the dish and its category, and shows you patterns in what you have logged.

## 3. What Forkast is not

**This is the most important clause in this document. Read it.**

Forkast is not a medical device, and it is not a source of medical, nutritional,
dietary, or health advice. Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, a treatment, or a
recommendation from a qualified professional.

- **The calorie figures are estimates**, produced from the name of a dish and
  the category you filed it under. They are not measurements. They are not
  derived from laboratory analysis, from a manufacturer's nutrition label, or
  from weighing anything. Two plates of the same dish can differ by a large
  margin and Forkast cannot tell them apart.
- **The meal plans are generated by a language model.** They are suggestions
  assembled by software, not by a dietitian. They may be wrong, unsuitable for
  you, or occasionally nonsensical.
- **Forkast does not know anything about you medically.** It does not know your
  allergies, your medications, your conditions, whether you are pregnant, or
  whether you have a history of disordered eating.

Do not use Forkast to manage a medical condition. Do not change your diet in a
way that matters on the strength of a number it showed you. **Talk to a doctor
or a registered dietitian.** If you are worried about your relationship with
food or your eating, please speak to a professional; a calorie counter is not
the right tool and may make things worse.
