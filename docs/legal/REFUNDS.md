## As things stand, there is nothing to refund

Forkast is free. There is no purchase price, no subscription, no in-app
purchase, and no advertising. Nothing in the app can charge you, and no payment
details are ever collected.

This is verifiable rather than a promise: the app contains no billing code and
no payment library. `expo-in-app-purchases`, RevenueCat, Stripe and every
equivalent are absent from its dependencies.

So this policy exists for two reasons: because both app stores expect one to be
reachable, and so that it is already written if Forkast ever does charge for
something.

## If you were charged anything

You should not have been. If a charge appears that you did not expect, tell us
at [[FILL: email address]] and we will help you get it back.

Where a charge went through Apple or Google rather than through us, they hold
the money and they process the refund, so the fastest route is directly to them:

- **Apple:** reportaproblem.apple.com
- **Google Play:** play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions, or Google's
  refund request form

We will support a request to either of them if you ask.

## If Forkast starts charging

Nothing will ever be charged without you agreeing to it first, and the free
features you already use will not be taken away and sold back to you.

Two statutory rights you cannot write your way out of, which is why this needs
a lawyer rather than a template:

  - **EU and UK: a 14 day right of withdrawal** on distance contracts for
    digital services. You may ask the consumer to waive it in order to get
    immediate access, but the waiver must be express and informed, and if you
    do not obtain it properly the 14 days stand.
  - **Australia, and several other jurisdictions:** consumer guarantees that
    override a stated no-refund policy entirely.

Also note that Apple and Google set their own refund windows and will issue
refunds under them whatever this document says.]]

## Deleting your account

Deleting your account is free, immediate, and does not require contacting
anybody: **Profile → Delete my account**.

Because the app is free, deleting costs you nothing and refunds nothing. It does
erase everything permanently, so read the Privacy Policy section on deletion
first.
